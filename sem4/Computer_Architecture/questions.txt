# Computer Architecture — Sorted Question Bank
**PU · BE Computer Engineering · 4th Semester · Exams 2018–2025**

**How to read this file**

- **New** = new syllabus (2024 onwards). **Old** = old syllabus (2018–2025).
- Every unique question appears **once**, followed by every exam it appeared in (newest first). Near-identical wordings are merged, and compound questions are split into their separate topics.
- In every part, questions asked in the **new syllabus come first**, then questions asked only in the old syllabus.
- Question numbers were missing in most scans, so they were assigned by order (1a, 1b … 6b). A repeated question sits under the part where it most recently appeared in the new syllabus (or in the old syllabus if it was never asked in the new one).
- Where a question came with an *OR* option, both options are listed as separate questions.

**Unique questions:** 84 (Q1–Q6) + 30 short-note topics (Q7) = 114
